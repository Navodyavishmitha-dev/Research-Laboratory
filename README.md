## 01. Laboratory Mission

The Research Laboratory is a personal scientific workspace dedicated to studying fundamental scientific principles, exploring technological possibilities, investigating questions, and documenting the journey of discovery.

Its mission is to develop scientific knowledge through structured learning, critical thinking, experimentation, and evidence-based investigation.

## 03. Research Methodology

Every investigation should follow a systematic process:

1. **Observation** — Identify a phenomenon or problem.
2. **Question** — Formulate a clear research question.
3. **Background Study** — Review existing scientific knowledge.
4. **Hypothesis** — Develop a testable explanation when appropriate.
5. **Experimentation** — Design and conduct experiments.
6. **Analysis** — Examine collected data and observations.
7. **Conclusion** — Report findings and limitations.
8. **Documentation** — Preserve methods, evidence, and results.

## 04. Laboratory Principles

- Scientific honesty and intellectual integrity.
- Evidence before assumptions.
- Curiosity and critical thinking.
- Reproducible experiments wherever possible.
- Respect for established scientific knowledge.
- Continuous learning and improvement.

## 05. Research Documentation

This laboratory will maintain records of:

- Research notes and literature reviews.
- Mathematical and scientific studies.
- Experimental procedures and results.
- Programming experiments and simulations.
- Technical prototypes and engineering projects.
- Original ideas, questions, and hypotheses.

## 06. Long-Term Vision

To build the scientific knowledge, research capabilities, and engineering skills necessary to contribute to humanity through original discoveries and advanced technologies.

The long-term exploration includes artificial intelligence, physics, robotics, neuroscience, and space technology.
