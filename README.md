### Our Mission

The Research Laboratory is an independent scientific exploration workspace dedicated to understanding the universe, investigating scientific principles, and exploring the boundaries of technology.

This laboratory serves as a place to study, question, experiment, document, and develop ideas across multiple scientific disciplines.

Our mission is to build strong foundations in science and engineering while gradually developing the knowledge and capabilities required for original research and technological innovation.

</div>

Every research investigation should follow a systematic scientific process:

1. Identify an interesting phenomenon or problem.
2. Formulate a research question.
3. Investigate existing scientific knowledge.
4. Develop a testable hypothesis where appropriate.
5. Design and conduct experiments.
6. Analyze observations and collected data.
7. Evaluate results and limitations.
8. Document findings transparently.
